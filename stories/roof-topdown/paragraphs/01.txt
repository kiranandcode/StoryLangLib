The lab had gone quiet in the way it did after nine, just the fume hood breathing and the centrifuge ticking as it cooled. Kiran was relabeling sample tubes she had already labeled once when the door clicked and Theo came back in, coat still on, a little out of breath, as if he'd made the decision to return halfway down the stairs and taken them two at a time. "Forgot a cable," he said, not looking anywhere a cable might be. She knew the bench he'd used all month was bare; she had watched him clear it. He leaned against the doorframe and smiled at her with no hurry in it. "Also, you're the only reason this building is any good after dark. Seemed wrong to leave without saying so." Kiran felt the words land somewhere under her sternum and stay.
