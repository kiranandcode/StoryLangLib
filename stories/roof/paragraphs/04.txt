Kiran came back with her tea to an empty lab. The desk beside hers was bare, the chair pushed in, and she stood holding the mug, annoyed at how fast her stomach had dropped. Of course he had gone. People did. Then she saw the note, folded square on her keyboard. She put the mug down and opened it. Roof. Eleven. Like before. Before was the garage roof they used to climb at eleven at night when they were twelve, passing a torch between them and talking until the sky went grey. She read the last line twice, then a third time, slowly, as if it might change.
