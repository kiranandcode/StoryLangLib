"I'm making tea," Kiran said, already at the door. "Back in five. Don't touch anything." It sighed shut behind her. Theo stood in the quiet she'd left and listened to her footsteps go down the corridor, then the kettle's first rattle through the wall. He had meant to say it here, under the hood's drone, and he could see now that he wouldn't. She would come back, set her mug parallel to the tube rack, and he would end up talking about the spectrometer. He tore a corner off a sheet of weighing paper and wrote with her pen, because it was there: *Roof. Eleven. There's something I should have said. —T.* He read it twice, decided it was honest enough, and laid it on the shared bench between her tubes and his inside-out gloves. Then he took the stairs two at a time, up toward the rain.
