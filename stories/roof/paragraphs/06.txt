The stairwell smelled of dust and somebody's old coffee. She took the stairs two at a time, the way he had, then made herself slow down on the last flight. She was not going to arrive out of breath and looking like she cared. The door at the top stuck, as it always did. She shouldered it open and the rain blew in, and the city opened out below her, all those lit windows full of people with lives. Ran was by the low wall with his hands in his pockets and his hair already soaked. He turned when he heard the door, and he did not look surprised to see her at all.
