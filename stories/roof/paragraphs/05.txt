Kiran came back with two mugs, because she had decided on the stairs that bringing him one was not a confession, and found Mara on her stool instead, coat dripping onto the lino. Theo was gone. Mara held up a triangle of weighing paper like a winning ticket. "He asked me up," she said. "The roof, at eleven. He likes me, Kiran. I think he's liked me since Thursday." Her cheeks were pink from the cold or from this. Kiran set both mugs down side by side and lined them up with the tube rack. Of course. The walk to the station, the turnstile; Mara had told her about the turnstile. "That's wonderful," she said, and it came out sounding almost like her own voice. She wrapped her hands around the mug meant for him. It was too hot to hold, and she held it anyway.
