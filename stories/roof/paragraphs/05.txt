Something he should have said. She snorted at the bare desk. Knowing him, it would be that he had let her win at everything when they were twelve. It was a goodbye for old times' sake, a tidy one, before he left for good. She was not going to stand in a lab at this hour building a cathedral out of one line of his handwriting. She had built plenty of those before, out of less, for people who never noticed. Still, it was his last night. She folded the note into her pocket and told herself she was only going up because it would be rude not to.
