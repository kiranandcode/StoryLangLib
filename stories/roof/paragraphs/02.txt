"Don't flatter yourself," Kiran said. "I'm counting the hours until you're gone. Tomorrow I get my corner back, and nobody hums at the printer." She meant none of it, and heard exactly how much she meant none of it. Ran didn't take the bait. He tipped his head as though she had said something kind, and a grin opened slowly across his face. "You'll miss me," he said. "You did last time." "Not even a little." She saved the lemma into the wrong file and had to cut it out again, and he laughed, softly, as if she had answered a different question.
