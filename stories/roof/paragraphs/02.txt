"Don't flatter yourself," Kiran said. "I'm counting the hours until you're gone. Tomorrow I get my bench back, and nobody hums at the centrifuge." She meant none of it, and heard exactly how much she meant none of it. Theo didn't take the bait. He tipped his head as though she had said something kind, and a grin opened slowly across his face. "You'll miss me," he said. "Not even a little." She pressed a label onto the wrong tube and had to peel it off again, and he laughed, softly, as if she had answered a different question.
