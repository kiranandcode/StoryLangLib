He took his time, turning the mint over on his tongue. "Are you going to miss me?" It was such a bad first question that she nearly laughed. She capped the pen, set it parallel to the tube rack, and finally looked up. "Theo. You hum when the hood is loud. You leave your gloves inside out on my bench. You call the spectrometer 'she.'" She counted the offenses off on her fingers, though her hand was not entirely steady. "I've had tomorrow circled on the calendar for weeks." The rain ticked against the window. He studied her face, and whatever he found there pulled at the corner of his mouth, then the rest of it, until he was grinning at her as if she had confessed something. "Weeks," he said. "That's a long time to spend thinking about me."
