By ten the lab had gone quiet except for the fume hood's long exhale and the tick of the centrifuge cooling down. Kiran was relabeling tubes she had already labeled when the door clicked and Theo came back in, coat on, hair damp from the rain. "Forgot a cable," he said, and did not look for one. He leaned against the bench across from hers, close enough that she could smell wet wool and the mint he was always chewing. She kept her eyes on her pen. "It's probably under your laptop," she said. "Probably." He didn't move. "I fly out tomorrow afternoon, and I realized I never asked you anything that wasn't about the spectrometer." Her ears went hot. She wanted, absurdly, to know what he would have asked first. "So ask," she said, quieter than she meant.
