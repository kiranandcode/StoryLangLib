At ten the lab was down to two sounds, the fume hood breathing and Kiran's pen on the tube labels. Then the door clicked, and Theo came back in with rain on his shoulders. "Forgot a cable," he said, and did not look for one. He took the stool across her bench, the one nobody used, and watched her write. She lost count of the tubes. "Your last night," she said, "and you spend it here." "The company's better than the hotel bar." He said it plainly, the way he said everything, and smiled at her until she had to look up.
