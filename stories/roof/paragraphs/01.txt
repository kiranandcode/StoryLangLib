At ten the lab was down to two sounds, the fume hood breathing and Kiran's pen on the tube labels. Then the door clicked, and Ran came back in with rain on his shoulders. "Forgot my scarf," he said, and did not look for it. He took the stool across her bench, the one he had claimed all week, and watched her write. She lost count of the tubes. "Your last night," she said, "and you spend it here." "Fifteen years, Kiran. The hotel bar can wait." He said it plainly, the way he had said everything since he was nine, and smiled at her until she had to look up.
