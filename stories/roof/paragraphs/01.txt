At ten the lab was down to Kiran and the printers ticking as they cooled. Then the door clicked, and Ran came back in with rain on his shoulders. "Forgot my scarf," he said, and did not look for it. He took the desk beside hers, the one he had borrowed all week, and watched her type. She lost her place in the proof. "Your last night," she said, "and you spend it here." "Fifteen years, Kiran. The hotel bar can wait." He said it plainly, the way he had said everything since he was nine, and smiled at her until she had to look up.
