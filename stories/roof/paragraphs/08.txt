"Kiran," he said. "Can I kiss you?" Nobody had ever asked her that as though it were a real question, with the answer still open. She could have made a joke. She had one ready, something about him taking ten years to get to the point. Instead she heard herself say "Yes," and then, because she was still herself, "Only if you're sure. I'm not doing this as a goodbye." "I'm sure," he said. "Yes. Not a goodbye."
