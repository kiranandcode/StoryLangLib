She was the one who closed the distance, because if she waited she would talk herself out of it. She took his face in her hands and kissed him. He was warm and tasted faintly of the rain, and he made a small surprised sound against her mouth and then held her as though he had been planning where his hands would go for ten years. When she pulled back to breathe, he kept his forehead against hers, and for once in her life she did not reach for the joke. She let him hold her, and below them the city went on without them.
