AI-first Verified Tooling (in progress) – Verification and language tooling are built for and around the limitations of human users. Anecdotally, it seems like the strengths and weaknesses of LLMs, while similar, are not always the same as humans, so maybe it might be possible to adapt the interfaces of tools to make them more amenable to the jagged intelligence of agents, for example, by exposing more details about tooling internals, such as the instantiation graph of an SMT solver etc. We're running some experiments with Amazon to try improving the capabilities of LLMs in using such verification tools by exposing these details.
