Developing semantics for new languages (in progress) – Colleagues at my work developed a new language for dynamical systems (dynestyx). They were not programming languages researchers but we helped them to build a really cool new language which unified a TON of work in their area. This language lacks a formal semantics, and this would be something I would be extremely qualified to help them set up, but I am not an expert in their domain, nor do we really have the time to sit down and slowly get me up to speed in their area. Instead, as a new form of collaboration, I have been experimenting using LLMs to automatically generate mechanised big step semantics for their language, validate it with testing, and then working with them to iterate this semantics to match their intent.
