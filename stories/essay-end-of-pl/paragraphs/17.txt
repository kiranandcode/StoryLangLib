Ship your interpreters — LLMs are incredibly capable of handling and managing tedious and boring proofs, at a scale no human would ever have done themselves. If we point an LLM at a problem, they can totally verify programs to the binary level. By exploiting this capability I ran an experiment of automatically generating semantics for interpreted languages, by getting the LLM to generate a semantics, and then prove that it abstracts the behaviours of the concrete binary of the language itself. This idea would have been absolutely unthinkable a year ago, but today? today it's just a month of prompting.
