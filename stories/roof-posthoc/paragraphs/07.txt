The roof was colder than Mara expected, the wind coming off the river with rain in it, and Theo stood by the railing with his hands in his pockets. He turned when the door banged, and she watched his face change: not displeasure, only surprise, and then a careful kindness that told her before he spoke. Still, she held up the note. "Was this for me?" Theo took a breath. "No," he said gently. "I'm sorry. It was for Kiran. I should have put her name on it." Mara felt her cheeks go hot and then, oddly, settle. She thought of Kiran's too-careful voice downstairs, the two mugs. "Oh," she said, and then, almost laughing, "oh, she's an idiot. Wait here."
