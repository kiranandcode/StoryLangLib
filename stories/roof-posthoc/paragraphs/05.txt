Kiran came back holding two mugs, because she had made one for him without deciding to, and found Mara instead of Theo, glowing by the bench with a sticky note held up like a ticket. "He likes me," Mara said. "Theo. Look. He's asked me up to the roof." Kiran read it over her friend's shoulder. The handwriting was his, square and slanted. She set both mugs down very carefully, so neither would clatter. Of course. Mara was easy to like; Mara asked people questions and meant them. "That's lovely," Kiran said, and her voice came out almost right. She looked at the second mug steaming by her elbow and felt something in her chest fold closed like a hand. "You should go."
