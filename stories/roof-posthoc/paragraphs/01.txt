By half past ten the lab had gone quiet in the way Kiran liked best: the fume hoods humming, the overhead panels dimmed to their night setting, the only real light pooled over her bench. She was relabelling sample tubes in her smallest handwriting when the door clicked and Theo came back in, coat already on, keys in his hand. "Forgot a cable," he said, and did not look for one. He leaned against the end of her bench instead, close enough that she could smell the cold he'd carried in from the corridor. "You know you're the only person in this building who stays later than me." He said it the way he said everything, as if there were no reason not to. "I was hoping you'd still be here."
