Mara had got as far as the bus stop before she realised her phone was at eleven percent and her charger was still plugged in under her desk. She let herself back into the lab, humming, and saw the yellow square at once, sitting on the shared bench halfway between her monitor and Kiran's. *Roof. Eleven. There's something I should have said.* She read it twice. Her stomach turned over, pleasantly. Theo had sat beside her at Thursday's seminar and laughed at her joke about the vacuum pump, and twice this week he had asked what she was working on and listened to the whole answer. She pressed the note flat against her palm. It was ten forty. She wanted, quite simply, to know what he would say.
