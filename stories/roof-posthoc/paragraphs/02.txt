Kiran capped her pen. Her pulse had done something unhelpful, and she wanted very much for him not to see it. "I'm here because it's the only time you aren't," she said. "Tomorrow I get the place back. I've been counting down." She turned a tube in her fingers and read its label as though it might have changed. "Honestly, I can't stand you. You hum when you solder." Theo's grin arrived slowly and then all at once, and it was not the grin of a man who had been insulted. "I do hum," he said. "Badly." He looked at her a beat longer than the joke needed, and she understood that he had heard every word backwards, exactly as she'd meant them, and that he was pleased.
