"I'm making tea," Kiran said, standing too quickly. "Don't touch anything. Two minutes." The door sighed shut behind her and Theo was alone with the hoods and the familiar sense that he had been about to say something and had swallowed it again, as he had every evening for three weeks. Tomorrow he flew home. He found a yellow sticky pad by the centrifuge and wrote before he could argue himself out of it: *Roof. Eleven. There's something I should have said. —T.* He set it on the long bench she shared with Mara, squared to the edge where she would see it walking back. Then, because waiting in the room while she read it was more than his nerve would hold, he took the stairs up.
