When the door opened again Kiran did not look up, because she did not trust her face. Mara crouched by her stool so they were eye to eye, wind-flushed, the note still in her hand. "It was for you," she said. "He told me. It was always for you. I just picked it up." Kiran stared at the yellow square. She thought of every dry thing she had said tonight and how he had grinned at each one. "Are you all right?" she asked, because that mattered first. Mara smiled, a little rueful. "I'll survive. I'll want details." The fear was still there, but under it curiosity had already begun its slow climb, and something warmer behind that. Kiran stood. "I'm going up."
