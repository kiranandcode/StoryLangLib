Kiran came back with the coffee to a bare desk on her left. Then Mara came in behind her, back for her charger, her hair still wet. She saw the folded page on the shelf, picked it up and read it twice, and her whole face changed. "Kiran. He's asked me up to the roof." She held it out like evidence. "Theo. He wants me." Something dropped under Kiran's ribs, a fast, clean fall with no cause she could find. She noted that too, and said nothing; she had nothing yet. Mara waited, then laughed at herself. "This is where you tell me I'm not imagining this." "You're not," Kiran said. "He asked about your thesis twice this week. That's above baseline."
