"Your note had no addressee," Kiran said, holding it up. "Mara read it as hers. Was it for me?" "It was for you." He winced. "I told her. I'm sorry she got the sharp end of it." He didn't look away. "I want you, Kiran. I came back four nights running for you." She watched his face while he spoke, the way she would watch a trace for a glitch. It came back clean. She gave him her data in return: her pulse when he leaned over the board, the lemma she lost, the fall under her ribs at "He wants me." "I can't make the effect go away by explaining it," she said. "I've tried two hypotheses." "I've got a third," Theo said.
