Ten minutes later Mara was back, red to the ears and laughing at herself. "It wasn't for me." Up on the roof, she said, Theo had told her as kindly as anyone could that the note was for Kiran. She put it in Kiran's hand. "For you, you absolute empiricist." Kiran read it: no name, only the initial. A badly specified message; she would have rejected it in review. "Also," Mara said, "you've eaten dinner alone at that desk every night for three years. You need people, Kiran. Go up." Kiran filed that as a claim from a reliable source, not something she could check from inside. Mara was still pink. "And I'm mortified. Say something nice to me." Kiran considered. "You read an ambiguous message under a plausible prior," she said. "That's not foolish. That's Bayes." Mara laughed properly then, and pushed her toward the stairs.
