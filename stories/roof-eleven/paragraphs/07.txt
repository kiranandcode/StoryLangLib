She took the stairs at an even pace. On the last flight the pace stopped being even. The door at the top stuck. She put her shoulder into it, and the cold came in all at once, a hard wind, the whole city lit below like a cluster under load. Theo stood in the lee of the water tank with his coat collar up. He turned when the door banged shut behind her. He did not look surprised, only glad, and she had no model for that either.
