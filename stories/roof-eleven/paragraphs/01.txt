At twenty past ten the lab was down to Kiran, one lemma that would not close, and the cluster humming at the far end. Then Theo came in behind her with his coat still on and two cartons of noodles in one hand. "The fans on node six," he said. "Thought I'd check." He did not go near the rack. It was the fourth night running, and four made a pattern; she logged the count, the way she would log any effect that kept replicating. He set a carton by her laptop. "You haven't eaten since this morning, Kiran." She checked, and he was right. She had been present the whole time and not noticed. "Fuel," she said, and ate. The noodles were still hot.
