At five to eleven Mara checked her face in the dark window, pushed her wet hair back twice, and went up with the note in her pocket. Kiran turned back to the lemma. She wrote one line and deleted it. The fall under her ribs had not resolved. She ran it like any fault. Hunger was out; she had eaten. Fatigue was out; she had been tired all week without it. Every hypothesis failed on the timing: the effect had started at "He wants me," not a second before. She had no name for it. She wrote that down instead of the lemma.
