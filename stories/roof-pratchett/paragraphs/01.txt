By ten o'clock the lab had emptied of everyone but Kiran and the printers, which ticked as they cooled.* Then the door clicked, and Ran came back in with the rain on his shoulders. "Forgot my scarf," he said. He did not look for it. There is a kind of man who forgets a scarf on purpose, and such men are rarer than you would think, because most men only forget scarves. Ran took the desk beside hers, the one he had borrowed all week, and watched her type like a heron at a pond that has never once held a fish. "Your Last Night," she said, "and you spend it here." "Ten years, Kiran." He said it plainly, the way he had said everything since he was nine, and smiled at her until she had to look up.
* Printers are the only machines that understand Deadlines, which is not the same thing as sympathy.
