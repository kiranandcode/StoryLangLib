Kiran came back with her tea to an empty lab. The desk beside hers was bare and the chair was pushed in, and her stomach dropped like a lift that knows the way. Of course he had gone. People do.* Then she saw the note on her keyboard. She put the mug down and opened it. Roof. Eleven. Like before. Before was the garage roof they used to climb at eleven at night when they were twelve, passing a flashlight between them and talking until the sky went grey. She read the last line twice, then a third time, slowly, as if it might change.
* This is one of the Great Truths, and like most Great Truths holds until somebody comes back up the stairs.
