"Kiran," he said. "Can I kiss you?" Nobody had ever asked her that as though it were a real question, with the answer still open. Most people treat a kiss like a parcel, to be signed for later by somebody.* She could have made a joke. She had one ready, something about him taking ten years to get to the point. Instead she heard herself say "Yes," and then, because she was still herself, "Only if you're sure. I'm not doing this as a goodbye." "I'm sure," he said. "Yes. Not a goodbye."
* Asking is not the dull part. Asking is the part where two people find out that the story is the same story for both, which is the most exciting thing that can happen to anybody.
