Something he should have said. Kiran snorted at the bare desk. Knowing him, it would be that he had let her win at everything when they were twelve. It was Old Times, that was all: a goodbye with a bow on it, before he left for good. She was not going to stand in a lab at this hour building a cathedral out of one line of his handwriting. She had built plenty of those before, out of less, for men who never noticed the incense.* Still, it was his last night. She folded the note into her pocket and told herself she was only going up because it would be rude not to, which is the sort of excuse that has carried more people up more staircases than love has.
* Humans build cathedrals out of anything. Two stones and a sideways look are enough, and by morning there is a choir.
