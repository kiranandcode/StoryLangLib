# Sports Illustrated's AI-generated author bios

The profiles of "Drew Ortiz" and "Sora Tanaka", product-review authors on
Sports Illustrated's site whose headshots were sold as AI-generated images and
who had no other trace; the profiles and their articles were removed after
[Futurism's report](https://futurism.com/sports-illustrated-ai-generated-writers)
of 27 November 2023, which quotes both bios. Retrieved 2026-11-15.

The rewrite can remove the slop but not the fabrication: these people do not
exist.
