# Vanderbilt Peabody College email after the Michigan State shooting

Sent on 16 February 2023 by Vanderbilt University's Peabody College Office of
Equity, Diversity and Inclusion, three days after the shooting at Michigan
State University. It ended "(Paraphrase from OpenAI's ChatGPT AI language
model, personal communication, February 15, 2023)", and the office apologised
the next day. Body text as reproduced in full by Josh Bernoff,
[About that Vanderbilt post-shooting email](https://web.archive.org/web/2023/https://bernoff.com/blog/about-that-vanderbilt-post-shooting-email-chatgpt-feigns-sympathy-poorly-but-so-do-humans);
reported by the [Vanderbilt Hustler](https://vanderbilthustler.com/2023/02/17/peabody-edi-office-responds-to-msu-shooting-with-email-written-using-chatgpt/).
The salutation ("Dear Peabody Family:"), the ChatGPT citation and the
sign-off are left out. Retrieved 2026-11-15.
